Concepts of Playing Card games:

All groups of cards are arranged in collections.  Unless otherwise
stated, a collection is a FIFO sequence.  A collection can be empty.

Deck - a collection of cards, typically 52, shuffled and played from
off-screen; not visible in the game until cards from the deck is
played, usually as part of game setup.

Stack - A collection of cards arranged either vertically or
horizontally so that they overlap slightly.  Cards in a stack can be
face up or face down.

Pile - A collection of cards arranged in a single pile.  A pile can be
face up or face down.  Only the top card is displayed.  An indicator
of how many cards are in the pile may be displayed.

Draw Pile - A special pile, face down, which can be clicked to have
cards drawn from it.

Discard Pile - A special pile, face up, into which cards are discarded
as per the game rules.  You cannot interact with Discard Piles.
